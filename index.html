Privacy Policy

Passport Photo Maker respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Data Collection

Passport Photo Maker does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app does not require account registration, login, or personal details.

2. Photos and Image Processing

All photos selected or captured within the app are processed locally on your device. Images are not uploaded to any server and are not transmitted to the developer.

3. Camera and Storage Permissions

The app may request permission to access:

  • Camera – to capture a passport photo.
  • Storage / Photos – to save or select images.

These permissions are used only for the core functionality of the app and no data leaves your device.

4. Third-Party Services

Passport Photo Maker does not use advertising SDKs, analytics services, or third-party tracking tools.

5. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no personal data is collected, the app is safe for general audiences.

6. Data Security

Because the app does not transmit or store user data externally, your information remains under your control on your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if app functionality changes. Updates will be reflected on this page.
